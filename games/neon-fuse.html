How to play

Tap a wire to turn it

Each tap turns it a quarter, clockwise.

Keep the spark moving

It burns along connected wire. The glowing outline shows where it goes next.

Crossings go straight

One wire bridges over the other, so the current never turns there. Burn through both for a bonus; bulbs score extra too.

Dead ends fizzle

If the wire runs out you get a moment to fix it. Burnt wire grows back.

Mouse: click a wire to turn it. P pauses.
