How to play

Swipe to cut

Drag one straight line across the shape. Lift your finger to cut.

Aim for 50 / 50

The closer the two halves, the more points. Great and perfect cuts build a combo.

Mind the clock

Each shape gives you a few seconds. A sloppy cut or a timeout costs a life. Soon shapes start to spin and drift.
