How to play

Listen

The lanterns sound a short tune, one at a time. Low notes on the left, high on the right.

Play it back

Tap the lanterns in the same order. Keep the rhythm for an "in time" bonus.

Three flames

A wrong lantern costs a flame and the tune plays again. Clean rounds build a multiplier.
