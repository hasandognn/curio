How to play

Tap a bolt to lift

Its top nuts of one colour spin up the thread.

Tap another to screw them on

It must be empty or have the same colour on top, with room to spare.

One colour per bolt

A full bolt of one colour lights up. Light them all to clear the level.

Undo, extra bolt, hints

Each is limited. Restart is always free.

Keyboard: 1–9 pick a bolt, Z undoes, R restarts, H hints, P pauses
