How to play

Drag to turn the ring

The slot at the bottom, over the launcher, is where your shot goes.

Tap to fire

The orb flies in and lands on top of that column.

Three of a colour pop

Side by side or stacked. Blocks outside fall in and can chain.

Watch the core

Every few shots it grows a new ring and pushes everything out. Past the edge, it's over.

Keyboard: left and right turn, space fires, C swaps with next, P pauses
