How to play

Swipe to tilt

Every ball rolls that way at once, until a wall or another ball stops it.

Each ball to its own colour

A ball drops into a hole of its colour as it rolls over it. Other holes let it pass.

Fewer moves, more stars

Match par for three stars. Undo and restart are free; hints are limited.

Keyboard: arrow keys tilt, Z undoes, R restarts, P pauses
