How to play

Tap to flick

The frog sways side to side. Tap when its aim lines up with a firefly.

Line them up

One tongue can catch several. Each one in the chain is worth more.

Keep the night going

Night slips away. Catches bring it back, misses cost a little. At dawn the round ends.

Leave the moths

A moth stops your tongue and costs night. Gold fireflies make the pond glow: double points.
