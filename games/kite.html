How to fly

Drag down to pull

A firm line climbs. Ease up and the kite drifts. Let it go slack and it dives.

Slide to steer

Lean left or right. Hold it to the side and the kite loops: style points.

Feel the gusts

Grass, petals and streaks show a gust coming. Ease off before it hits, or the line goes red and snaps.

Ribbons, lanterns, rings

Collect and chain them. Birds, trees and other kites' lines tear a bow off your tail. Three bows.
