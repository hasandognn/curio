How to play

Drag left or right

The piece follows your finger, one column at a time.

Tap to rotate

Tap with two fingers to rotate the other way.

Drag down, or flick to drop

A slow drag eases it down. A quick flick slams it to the floor.

Flick up to hold

Saves the piece for later. Tapping the Hold box works too.

Keyboard: arrows move, up or X rotates, Z rotates back, space drops, C holds, P pauses
