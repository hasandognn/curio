How to play

Swipe to send a wave

The tide rushes that way and carries every creature with it, each by its own rule.

Stop them on their own pool

A creature that stops on a pool of its colour settles in for good, and then stops others. Passing over does not count.

Waves are counted

Settle everyone before the tide runs out. Match par for three stars. Undo is free.

Tap a creature

It tells you how it moves.

Keyboard: arrow keys send waves, Z undoes, R restarts, P pauses
